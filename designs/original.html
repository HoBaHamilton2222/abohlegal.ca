ABOUT THE FIRM

A litigator who listens first.

With a legal journey spanning from the vibrant courts of Lagos to the vast landscapes of Alberta, ABOH LEGAL brings a unique, global perspective to local advocacy.

Our founder is driven by a simple philosophy: clear communication is the foundation of every successful case. We cut through the legalese to provide you with a straightforward path forward, ensuring you are never left in the dark.

As a boutique firm, we prioritize quality over volume. When you hire us, you work directly with your lawyer—not a paralegal or junior associate.
